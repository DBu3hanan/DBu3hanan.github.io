Jamaica Is Not Angry Enough About Pedophilia 

Diamond A. Buchanan | Dabuchanan0325@gmail.com

August 4, 2026 | Kingston, Jamaica

Reading about the children in Jamaica whose lives have been tragically cut short by viscous animals who happened to be classified as humans will never cease to both anger and radicalize me. If you search up the phrase "child in Jamaica who was assaulted and killed" in any search engine, the plethora of articles that pop up SHOULD bring anyone to tears.

The newest story on the block seems to be the saga of the 10 year old girl who was raped and murdered in Trelawny back in July of this year. She was found partially nude with her throat slashed on a dirt track in bushes, her name is Shani Coke. When the news hit all the local newspapers the shock and horror it came with was unfortunately not as morbid as it should be. 

Instead all the horror was preserved when I open the comments section on one of the story post to read peoples opinions on the matter. I could not believe that there were people blaming the guardians of the child for her murder or even worst, blaming the deceased for her untimely demise. Apparently in their opinion the child should not have been outside in the first place and blame her guardians for putting her in harms way.

When you get near the young of a wild animal, majority of the time the mother attacks the element that is threatening her baby, rarely does she feature any anger towards the child.
